Privacy & Email Preferences

Property Growth Scanner by GrowTell collects only the information you choose to provide through the signup form, primarily your email address and the signup source.

How your email is used

Your email may be used to send you GrowTell growth-area discoveries, land-release updates, product updates and information about the planned Investor Pro service. Your email is submitted through Formspree and is not intentionally sold to third parties.

Unsubscribe

You can unsubscribe from GrowTell emails at any time. Until automated mailing-list unsubscribe links are enabled, reply to any GrowTell email with the word UNSUBSCRIBE and your address will be removed from future GrowTell marketing emails.

Third-party services

The signup form uses Formspree to receive form submissions. Formspree processes the information submitted through the form under its own terms and privacy practices.

Research disclaimer

Property Growth Scanner provides research signals and planning information for further investigation. It does not provide personal financial advice and does not guarantee property growth, infrastructure delivery or investment returns.
